The labels located in this user manual and on the machine body are placed in accordance with international standards in order to maintain operator safety at the highest level, indicate potential risks, and demonstrate the correct operating conditions of the machine.

Before commissioning the machine, performing maintenance, or intervening in heavy/hot parts, be sure to review the meanings of the following symbols and the mandatory actions they require.

| Symbol (Label) | Category | Description | Required Action / Instruction |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| ![General Hazard](../assets/labels/warning/warning1.svg) | **Warning** | General Hazard / Caution | Indicates potential hazardous areas on the machine or equipment. Act with caution in the work area and comply with the relevant additional safety instructions. |
| ![Hot Surface](../assets/labels/warning/warning2.svg) | **Warning** | Hot Surface Contact Hazard | There are surfaces on or around the machine that reach high temperatures. To prevent burn risks, do not touch these points with bare hands before the system has cooled down completely. |
| ![Corrosive Substance](../assets/labels/warning/warning3.svg) | **Warning** | Corrosive / Caustic Substance | Indicates areas where corrosive chemicals are present or used. Avoid direct contact with chemicals and take precautions against spill/splash risks. |
| ![Crush Hazard](../assets/labels/warning/warning4.svg) | **Warning** | Crush / Entrapment Hazard | Indicates areas with moving mechanisms where there is a risk of limb entrapment/crushing. Do not open protective guards while the machine is operating and stay away from moving parts. |
| ![Slippery Surface](../assets/labels/warning/warning5.svg) | **Warning** | Slippery Surface Hazard | There is a risk of slippery surfaces in the work area due to liquid spills or leaks. Proceed carefully against the hazard of falling. |
| ![Electric Shock](../assets/labels/warning/warning7.svg) | **Warning** | Electric Shock Hazard | Only authorized electrical personnel may open the electrical cabinet cover. Apply the LOTO procedure at the main switch before any intervention. |
| ![No Flammable Substances](../assets/labels/warning/warning6.jpg) | **Prohibition** | Flammable Substance / Combustible Material Prohibited | The use of flammable or explosive liquids and open flames in or around the machine is strictly prohibited. Use only substances approved by the manufacturer. |
| ![Read the Manual](../assets/labels/kkd/kkd1.webp) | **Mandatory** | Refer to Instruction Manual | Read the user manual completely and follow the instructions before commissioning, putting into operation, or performing any maintenance on the machine. |
| ![Protective Overalls](../assets/labels/kkd/kkd2.svg) | **PPE** | Mandatory Use of Workwear | It is mandatory to wear appropriate workwear or protective overalls to protect the body against potential risks during operation and maintenance processes. |
| ![Safety Shoes](../assets/labels/kkd/kkd3.svg) | **PPE** | Steel-Toe Shoes Mandatory | It is mandatory to use steel-toe protective safety shoes in the work area against falling objects, impact, or crushing risks. |
| ![Protective Gloves](../assets/labels/kkd/kkd4.svg) | **PPE** | Mandatory Use of Protective Gloves | It is mandatory to use protective gloves suitable for the nature of the job when handling sharp-edged, hot, or chemical-contaminated surfaces/parts. |
| ![Safety Glasses](../assets/labels/kkd/kkd5.svg) | **PPE** | Mandatory Use of Safety Glasses | It is mandatory to wear appropriate safety glasses to protect the eyes against liquid splashes, flying particles, or chemical vapor risks. |
| ![Respirator Mask](../assets/labels/kkd/kkd6.svg) | **PPE** | Mandatory Use of Respirator Mask | It is mandatory to use a filtered respirator mask suitable for the working environment against the risk of inhaling airborne harmful gases, vapors, or particles. |
| ![Earthing](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi1.svg) | **Information** | Protective Earth Connection Point | Indicates the protective earth (PE) connection point of the machine. Do not remove the earth connection; check that the connection is secure after maintenance and transport. |
| ![Keep Locked](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi2.svg) | **Information** | Keep Locked | Indicates covers that must be kept locked, such as the electrical cabinet cover. Keep the cover locked while the machine is operating; only authorized personnel use the key. |
| ![Drain](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi3.svg) | **Information** | Tank Drain Point | Indicates the drain valve used to empty the tanks. Check that the liquid has cooled down before draining; dispose of waste liquid in accordance with local environmental regulations. |
| ![Air Inlet](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi4.svg) | **Information** | Compressed Air Inlet | Indicates the compressed air connection point of the machine. Connect dry, filtered air at the pressure given in the technical data; shut off the air supply before maintenance. |
| ![Water Inlet](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi5.svg) | **Information** | Water Inlet | Indicates the mains water connection point of the machine. Close the water inlet valve before maintenance and before removing hoses. |
| ![Tank Filling](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi6.svg) | **Information** | Tank Filling Point | Indicates the point where water or washing chemical is filled into the tank. Fill the tank only from this point; wear protective gloves and glasses when adding chemicals. |
| ![Lock / Unlock](../assets/labels/bilgi/bilgi7.svg) | **Information** | Energy Lockout Point (LOTO) | Indicates the point where energy is isolated and locked before maintenance. Attach your personal padlock; only the person who attached the lock removes it. |

> **Note:** Immediately replace worn, removed, or illegible warning labels on the machine with new ones. Operating a machine without labels is a violation of occupational safety.
